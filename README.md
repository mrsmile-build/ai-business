# 📊 AI Business

**Find Customers. Win Them. Never Lose Them.**

AI Business is an all-in-one customer management and growth platform built for small businesses and entrepreneurs in Nigeria and across Africa.

🔗 **Live app:** https://www.ai-business.com.ng

## Features

- 🎯 **Lead Finder** – Find real local businesses with contact details: https://www.ai-business.com.ng/features/lead-finder
- 🤖 **AI Receptionist** – Answers customer questions 24/7: https://www.ai-business.com.ng/features/ai-receptionist
- 📅 **Appointments** – Self-service booking page: https://www.ai-business.com.ng/features/appointments
- ⏰ **Follow-Up Assistant** – AI-drafted follow-ups for cold leads: https://www.ai-business.com.ng/features/follow-up-assistant
- 🧾 **Invoicing** – Professional invoices with payment reminders: https://www.ai-business.com.ng/features/invoicing
