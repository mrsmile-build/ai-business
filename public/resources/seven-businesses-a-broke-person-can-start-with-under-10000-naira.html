7 Businesses You Can Start With Under ₦10,000

The real blocker isn't capital

People say "I have no money to start." The truth is usually "I have no first customer." Capital follows the first customer, not the other way round. Each business below starts under ₦10,000 because each one sells effort or skill first and buys stock only after someone has paid.

BusinessStarts withFirst-customer move

The pattern in every row

Sell first, buy second. Deliver one real job, get one line of proof, then charge. And put a public page behind it — name, area, open-now, real prices, one WhatsApp button — because the stranger who checks you and finds nothing buys from the person who has something.

You don't need ₦10,000 to start. You need one person to say yes. The ₦10,000 is what the first yes buys you.
