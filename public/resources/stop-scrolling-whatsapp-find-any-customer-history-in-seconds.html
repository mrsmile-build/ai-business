Stop Scrolling WhatsApp: How to Pull Up Any Customer's Full History in 10 Seconds

The scroll is where customers die

"Give me a sec, let me find our chat." You scroll. And scroll. Somewhere in an old thread is the message where she agreed to the price. By the time you surface, she's gone quiet — and you've looked disorganised doing it. The scroll isn't a small annoyance; it's the gap where trust leaks.

One box, everything about them

AI Business keeps every lead, note, proposal and booking in one searchable place. Type a name, a phone number, a word from the job — even a date like "what did I do on 16 Oct" — and the whole history surfaces, grouped by type and dated.

  • A name — "Mama Put" pulls every thread with that customer.
  • A phone number — the fastest way when you only have the contact.
  • A word from the job — "agbada", "inspection", "braids".
  • A date — "20 09 2026" shows everything that happened that day.
  • A feature — "appointments" or "invoice" jumps straight to that screen.

The habit that makes it work

Search is only as good as the record behind it. Save the enquiry the moment it arrives and the search has something to find. Ten seconds of saving now buys ten seconds of finding, forever.
