How to Manage Enquiries During a Product Launch – A Complete Guide

Introduction

Launching a new product is exhilarating, but it also brings a flood of questions, doubts, and excitement from potential customers. Every enquiry is a chance to build trust, nurture a lead, and ultimately convert interest into revenue. Yet, without a clear system, those messages can become chaotic, slow to answer, or even ignored – a missed opportunity that can hurt your launch momentum.

In this guide, we’ll walk you through how to manage enquiries during a product launch step‑by‑step. From building a centralised hub to automating replies, empowering your support team, and continuously optimising performance, you’ll walk away with a repeatable process that turns curiosity into sales.

> Pro tip: Treat each enquiry as a mini‑sales funnel. The faster and more accurately you respond, the higher the conversion rate.

---

1. Set Up a Centralised Enquiry Hub

Why a Single Destination Matters

During a launch, enquiries pour in from multiple channels – your landing page, social media ads, email newsletters, live chat, and even phone calls. If each channel lives in its own silo, you risk duplicated effort, missed messages, and inconsistent answers.

Choose the Right Platform

A centralised hub collects every inbound request in one place. Popular options include:

  • Freshdesk – ticketing system with multi‑channel support and robust automation.
  • Zendesk – powerful knowledge‑base integration and reporting.
  • Zoho Desk – cost‑effective for startups and integrates nicely with Zoho CRM.

All three offer free tiers that are sufficient for a launch‑phase volume.

Integrate Your Communication Channels

Connect the hub with:

  • Web forms – embed a form on your launch page hosted on Vercel (https://vercel.com/ or Render (https://render.com/
  • Social media DMs – use Zapier (https://zapier.com) or Make (https://www.make.com) to route Facebook, Instagram, and Twitter messages to your ticketing system.
  • Live chat – tools like Intercom or Tidio can push chats directly into Freshdesk tickets.
  • Phone – set up a virtual number with Namecheap (https://www.namecheap.com/ and forward call logs.

By funneling everything into a single queue, you gain visibility, ensure no enquiry slips through, and can assign tickets to the right team members instantly.

---

2. Automate Responses with Chatbots & Email Sequencing

Instant Acknowledgement Saves Credibility

When a prospect sends a query, the first few minutes are critical. An automated acknowledgement tells them you’re listening and sets expectations for a full response.

#### Example Auto‑Reply

```

Subject: Thanks for reaching out!

Hi {{first_name}},

We’ve received your question about {{product_name}} and will get back to you within 2‑4 hours. Meanwhile, feel free to check our FAQ: https://ai-business.com.ng/faq

Best,

The {{company}} Team

```

Chatbots for 24/7 Front‑Line Support

Deploy a chatbot on your launch site to answer the most common questions (price, availability, specifications). Tools like ManyChat (https://manychat.com) or ChatGPT‑powered bots on Cloudflare Workers (https://www.cloudflare.com/ can handle simple queries instantly, freeing human agents for complex issues.

Email Sequencing for Lead Nurture

After the initial reply, schedule a short sequence:

1. Hour 1: Detailed answer with a link to the product’s spec sheet.

2. Day 1: Case study or testimonial.

3. Day 3: Limited‑time launch offer (e.g., 10% off with Paystack (https://paystack.com/ or Flutterwave (https://flutterwave.com/ checkout).

Automation platforms like MailerLite (https://mailerlite.com) or ActiveCampaign (https://activecampaign.com) let you set these flows without writing code.

---

3. Leverage CRM & Ticketing Systems for Contextual Follow‑Up

Keep the Full Customer Journey Visible

A CRM stores every interaction – from the first enquiry to purchase and post‑sale support. When a ticket is created, automatically attach the prospect’s lead record. This gives agents context (e.g., previous purchases, marketing source) and enables personalised replies.

Recommended Stack

| Function | Tool | Why It Works |

|---|---|---|

| CRM | HubSpot CRM (free) | Centralised contact database, integrates with Freshdesk via Zapier |

| Ticketing | Freshdesk | Multi‑channel inbox, SLA management |

| Email Automation | MailerLite | Simple sequencing, easy to embed links |

| Payments | Paystack / Flutterwave | Local Nigerian payment gateways, seamless checkout |

Syncing Enquiries to the Sales Funnel

1. Create a lead in HubSpot when a ticket is opened.

2. Tag the lead with `Launch_Enquiry`.

3. Score leads based on urgency, product interest, and budget.

4. Trigger a sales‑rep notification in Slack (https://slack.com) for high‑score leads.

This workflow ensures hot prospects receive rapid, human follow‑up while low‑priority tickets are handled via automation.

---

4. Prepare Your Team with Scripts, FAQs, and Knowledge Base

Build a Live‑Document Knowledge Base

Use Notion (https://www.notion.so) or Confluence (https://www.atlassian.com/software/confluence) to compile:

  • Product specs, pricing tiers, and launch dates.
  • Answers to the top 20 most‑asked questions.
  • Sample scripts for handling objections (e.g., “Is the product available for pre‑order?”).

Make the knowledge base searchable and link it directly in Freshdesk tickets for quick reference.

Role‑Playing Sessions

Before the launch, run mock‑call sessions:

  • One agent plays the customer, another responds using the script.
  • Record the session, review for tone, clarity, and speed.
  • Refine scripts based on real‑world feedback.

Empower Agents with Decision‑Making Authority

Define clear SLA thresholds:

  • Standard enquiries – reply within 2 hours.
  • High‑value leads – reply within 30 minutes and offer a personal demo link.
  • Escalations – route to senior manager if the enquiry involves contract negotiations.

When agents know exactly when they can close a deal or offer a discount, response times improve dramatically.

---

5. Monitor, Analyse, and Optimise in Real Time

Real‑Time Dashboards

Create a dashboard in Google Data Studio (https://datastudio.google.com) that pulls data from Freshdesk, HubSpot, and your payment gateway. Track:

  • Ticket volume per hour.
  • First response time (FRT) and resolution time.
  • Conversion rate from enquiry to purchase.
  • Revenue attributed to each channel.

Set alerts for spikes (e.g., a sudden surge after a Facebook ad goes live) so you can add extra agents or boost automation.

A/B Test Messaging

Experiment with two versions of your auto‑reply:

  • Version A: Emphasises limited‑time discount.
  • Version B: Highlights product benefits and a video demo.

Measure which version drives higher click‑through to the checkout page (via Paystack or Flutterwave) and adjust accordingly.

Post‑Launch Review

After the launch window closes, conduct a debrief:

1. Quantify total enquiries, conversion rate, and revenue.

2. Identify bottlenecks – e.g., “Responses over 4 hours on weekends”.

3. Update FAQs and scripts based on new questions that emerged.

4. Document lessons learned for the next product release.

Continuous improvement turns each launch into a smoother, more profitable event.

Actionable Takeaway

> Implement a 3‑Step Enquiry Management Playbook for Your Launch:

> 1️⃣ Centralise all incoming messages in Freshdesk (or your chosen ticketing system).

> 2️⃣ Automate an instant acknowledgement and a 2‑step email sequence using MailerLite.

> 3️⃣ Assign high‑score leads (identified via HubSpot) to senior reps for a personal follow‑up within 30 minutes.

>

> Execute this playbook on day one of your launch and monitor the FRT dashboard. You’ll see response times drop by at least 40 % and conversion rates climb.
