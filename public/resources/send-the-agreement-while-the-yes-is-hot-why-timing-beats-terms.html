Send the Agreement While the Yes Is Hot

The yes has a shelf life

Here's the secret nobody tells new owners: the moment after a client says yes is the highest-trust moment they'll ever feel about you. It fades by the hour. Sleep on it and the excitement cools, the budget doubt creeps in, a competitor slides into their inbox. The agreement you send tomorrow faces a colder client than the one you send in the next ten minutes. Timing isn't a detail — it's most of the battle.

Good-and-now beats perfect-and-later

Slow and polishedFast and solid
Two days crafting the "right" wordingTen seconds generating a clean summary
Sent after the enthusiasm cooledSent while they're still smiling
Feels like a legal eventFeels like the natural next step
Client has had time to change their mindClient confirms before they can

The terms matter, but a perfect agreement that arrives after the yes has cooled is worth less than a fair one that arrives while it's hot. The goal isn't the flawless document — it's the locked document, fast.

The habit that makes it automatic

Decide in advance that the agreement goes out in the same conversation as the yes — not "I'll send it later." Later is where yeses go to die. If generating it takes a minute instead of an afternoon, you'll actually do it in the moment, and the deal survives the only window that matters.

You don't lose the client to a worse competitor. You lose them to the time between the yes and the signature.
