Your First 14 Days

Why a map beats a manual

New owners don't fail for lack of features. They fail because the dashboard has twelve buttons and no order. So here is the order — fourteen days, four phases, one job per morning. You don't need to understand the whole tool on day one. You need to do today's thing, then tomorrow's. The clarity you're waiting for arrives on day 14, not day 1 — it's a reward for moving, never a prerequisite.

The map

DaysPhaseThe one job each morning
1–3Set up your faceFill your profile, write your Voice Profile (how you actually talk), build your public Business Page with real prices. You're making the thing a stranger checks before they trust you.
4–7Find the buyersRun Lead Finder for your niche and city. Save ten real businesses a day. Don't message yet — just fill the board. Twenty to forty names by day 7.
8–11Open the conversationSend the warm first message (not a pitch — a question) to five a day. Log each reply. Set a follow-up date on every "let me think." This is where most owners quit; don't.
12–14Lock the yesGenerate a proposal for the warmest three. When one says yes, write the Agreement the same hour — before the yes cools. Then invoice. That's your first paying customer.

The rule that makes it work

One job per morning. Not "build a business" — "save ten names today." The size of the task is what defeats beginners; the smallness of today's task is what carries them. And when you want to scrap the plan on day 9 because replies are slow, remember the 24-hour rule: the silence on day 9 is the same silence that becomes a yes on day 13. Stay one day longer than the feeling asks.

Day 14 is not the finish line

By day 14 you'll have a page, a voice, a board of leads, a few live conversations, and — if you held the line — one locked yes and one invoice. That's not a small thing. That's a business that exists, built in two weeks by someone who was confused on day one. The next fourteen days are repetition: more leads, more follows, more locks. The machine you set up in week one runs itself from week two. You just keep feeding it mornings.

You don't need to feel ready on day 1. You need to do day 1's one job. Readiness is what day 14 looks like in hindsight.
