Why this matters more than a CRM

A provision shop does not lie awake wondering how to track a thousand buyers. It wonders why people walk past to the shop down the road. That is a visibility and trust problem, not a customer-management problem. The businesses that genuinely need enquiry tracking and follow-up are the ones that chase customers - consultants, agents, contractors, freelancers, wholesalers - and the ones with a few large repeat accounts.

So fix the order: first be findable, then be trusted, then be easy to buy from. Only after that does remembering individual customers start to pay.
