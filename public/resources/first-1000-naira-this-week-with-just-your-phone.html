How to Make Your First ₦1,000 This Week With Just Your Phone

Why ₦1,000 and not ₦1,000,000

Because the first sale is not about the money. It's about the proof. ₦1,000 from a real person for a real thing you did changes something inside you that no motivational speech ever will: you now know the thing you do has value. That knowledge is the seed of everything. So we're not chasing riches this week — we're chasing the first yes. The riches are a later chapter, and they only make sense after you've believed you're worth paying for.

This guide assumes you have nothing but a phone and a skill you don't even count as a skill — braiding, writing, cooking, fixing phones, driving, designing logos, teaching, cleaning, translating, running errands. If you have that, you have everything you need to start today.

The five steps, in order

  1. Name the one thing. Not your life's purpose. The single skill you can do well today. Write it down: "I can ______." That blank is your whole business for this week.
  2. Find five people who need it. Open your contacts, your WhatsApp groups, your church, your street, your market. Five humans who would pay for that thing. You don't need a website. You need five names.
  3. Send the first message. Short, warm, no pitch: "Hey, I do [the thing] — if you or anyone you know needs it this week, I'm taking orders." Send it to all five. Some will ignore you. That's fine. You need one.
  4. Do the job, charge the ₦1,000. Deliver well. Take the money. Say thank you. This is the moment the dream becomes possible — not because of the naira, but because a stranger just told you, with their wallet, that you matter.
  5. Ask for the next one. The person who paid you knows three people who need you. Ask. That's your second buyer, and now you're not starting — you're growing.

Where the tool fits (and where it doesn't)

You can do all five steps by hand today, and you should, at least once, so you feel the shape of it. The tool is for when the five become fifty and your hand can't hold the list anymore — it finds the buyers for you, remembers every one, drafts the message in your voice, and follows up the ones who go quiet so you never lose a yes to silence again. Start by hand. Let the machine take over the moment your hands are full.
