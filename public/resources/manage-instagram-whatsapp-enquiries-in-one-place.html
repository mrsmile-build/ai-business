How to Manage Instagram and WhatsApp Enquiries in One Place

Instagram and WhatsApp are two of the most powerful channels for customer acquisition in Africa. Yet many small‑to‑medium businesses still juggle two separate inboxes, miss messages, and lose sales. In this guide we’ll show you exactly how to manage Instagram and WhatsApp enquiries in one place, from choosing the right tools to setting up automation that saves you hours each week.

---

1. Why Consolidating Enquiries Matters for Your Bottom Line

| Pain Point | Impact on Business |

|------------|-------------------|

| Missed messages | Lost leads & revenue |

| Re‑typing responses | Wasted time (up to 30 % of a day) |

| No unified view of customer history | Inconsistent service |

| Manual follow‑ups | Higher churn |

When you treat Instagram DMs and WhatsApp chats as separate silos, you’re effectively splitting your sales funnel. A prospect who first contacts you on Instagram may later switch to WhatsApp for a quick chat – if you cannot see the earlier conversation, you’ll repeat information, look unprofessional, and risk losing the deal.

A unified inbox gives you:

  • Instant context – see every touch‑point, regardless of platform.
  • Faster response times – reply from a single dashboard.
  • Scalable workflows – automate FAQs, assign tickets, and track performance.
  • Data‑driven insights – measure conversion rates per channel.

All of these translate to higher conversion, happier customers, and more time for you to focus on growth.

3. Setting Up the Integration – Step‑by‑Step

Below is a practical, no‑code workflow using HubSpot CRM + ManyChat + Zapier (https://zapier.com). This combo works even if you don’t have developer resources.

3.1. Create a HubSpot Account & Enable the Inbox

1. Sign up at https://www.hubspot.com/products/crm.

2. Navigate to Conversations → Inbox.

3. Click Add channel, select Instagram and follow the OAuth flow to connect your Business Instagram account.

4. Repeat for WhatsApp – you’ll need a verified WhatsApp Business API provider (e.g., Twilio, MessageBird, or Gupshup). HubSpot will guide you through the verification.

3.2. Build a Simple FAQ Bot in ManyChat

1. Register at https://manychat.com and choose the Instagram + WhatsApp plan.

2. Use the Visual Flow Builder to create a “Welcome” block:

  • “Hi 👋! How can we help you today?”
  • Buttons: Pricing, Support, Talk to a Human.

3. Link each button to a pre‑written answer or to a HubSpot ticket via Zapier.

3.3. Connect ManyChat → HubSpot via Zapier

1. In Zapier, create a new Zap: Trigger = ManyChat – New Flow Trigger.

2. Action = HubSpot – Create Ticket.

  • Map the user’s phone number (WhatsApp) or Instagram username to the ticket’s contact field.
  • Add the message content to the ticket description.

3. Turn on the Zap. Now every Instagram DM or WhatsApp message that hits your ManyChat bot automatically appears as a ticket in HubSpot.

3.4. Centralised Reply from HubSpot

  • Open Conversations → Inbox.
  • You’ll see a combined view: Instagram chats on the left, WhatsApp threads on the right.
  • Click a ticket, type a reply, and HubSpot pushes it back to the original platform.
  • Use Snippets (canned responses) for pricing, delivery times, or payment instructions (e.g., “Pay via Paystack: https://paystack.com/

4. Automating Follow‑Ups and Conversions

Automation is the secret sauce that turns a unified inbox into a sales engine.

4.1. Triggered Follow‑Up Sequences

  • After a pricing inquiry: Send a WhatsApp template “Here’s our latest price list – let me know if you’d like a custom quote.” Use HubSpot’s Workflows to schedule the message 2 hours after the ticket is created.
  • Abandoned chat: If a prospect leaves the conversation without replying for >24 hours, automatically send a friendly nudge: “Hey, we noticed you didn’t finish your order. Need any help?”

4.2. Payment Integration

Because many Nigerian customers prefer local payment gateways, embed Paystack (https://paystack.com/ or Flutterwave (https://flutterwave.com/ links directly in your chat replies. Example:

```markdown

🔗 Pay now: https://paystack.com/

```

HubSpot can capture the payment status via webhook, automatically closing the ticket when payment is confirmed.

4.3. Reporting & KPIs

Set up a Dashboard in HubSpot to monitor:

  • Average response time (goal < 15 min).
  • Conversion rate per channel (Instagram vs. WhatsApp).
  • Ticket volume – identify peak hours and staff accordingly.
  • Revenue per conversation – link closed‑won deals to the originating ticket.

These metrics let you iterate on your bot flows, staffing, and promotional strategy.

5. Scaling the System – From 10 to 1,000 Daily Enquiries

When your business grows, the unified inbox must stay reliable and secure.

5.1. Load Balancing & Security

  • Host any custom middleware (e.g., extra Zapier steps, webhooks) on a Cloudflare‑protected server (https://www.cloudflare.com/ Cloudflare’s free tier offers DDoS protection and a global CDN, ensuring fast API calls to Instagram and WhatsApp.
  • Use Namecheap (https://www.namecheap.com/ to manage a short, brandable domain for your chat links (e.g., chat.yourbrand.ng). This improves trust and click‑through rates.

5.2. Team Collaboration

  • In HubSpot, enable Ticket Queues (e.g., “Instagram Leads”, “WhatsApp Support”). Assign agents automatically based on skill set.
  • Use @mentions within tickets to route complex queries to senior staff.
  • For remote teams, integrate HubSpot with Slack (https://slack.com) so agents receive instant notifications on new enquiries.

5.3. AI‑Powered Assistance

  • HubSpot’s AI Writer can suggest reply drafts based on previous tickets. Review and send – you cut reply time by up to 40 %.
  • For more advanced needs, plug in OpenAI GPT‑4 via a custom webhook on Vercel. The model can handle language translation (English ↔️ Yoruba, Igbo, Hausa) and sentiment analysis, flagging angry customers for priority handling.
