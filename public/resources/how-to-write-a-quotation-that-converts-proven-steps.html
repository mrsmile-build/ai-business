How to Write a Quotation That Converts – Proven Steps

How to Write a Quotation That Converts

> A great quotation is more than a price list – it’s a persuasive mini‑proposal that turns curiosity into a signed contract.

When you send a potential client a quotation, you are handing them a decision‑making tool. If it’s unclear, generic, or hard to read, the chances of conversion drop dramatically. In this guide we’ll walk you through every component of a high‑performing quotation, show you how to embed psychology, design for readability, and automate the workflow with the right tech stack.

---

1. Understand the Real Purpose of a Quotation

Most people treat a quotation as a simple price sheet. In reality, it’s a sales instrument that does three things simultaneously:

1. Validate the client’s problem – By restating the challenge you’ve identified, you reassure the prospect that you understand their need.

2. Show value, not just cost – Highlight outcomes, ROI, and differentiators.

3. Create a clear next step – A well‑structured quote ends with a call‑to‑action that makes it easy for the buyer to say yes.

1.1 The Buyer’s Decision Journey

| Stage | What the buyer thinks | What you must deliver |

|-------|-----------------------|-----------------------|

| Awareness | "Do they get me?" | Brief problem recap |

| Consideration | "Will this solve my issue?" | Detailed solution + benefits |

| Decision | "Is this easy to sign?" | Simple pricing, terms, and CTA |

When you align each section of your quotation with the buyer’s mental state, you dramatically increase conversion odds.

---

2. Core Elements of a High‑Converting Quotation

A quotation that converts follows a proven structure. Below is a checklist you can copy‑paste into any document editor.

2.1 Header – Trust Instantly

  • Your brand logo (hosted on a CDN like **[Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/ for fast load times)
  • Company name, address, and contact – Include a clickable phone link (`tel:`) and email (`mailto:`).
  • Quote reference number and date – Gives the document a professional feel and aids tracking.

2.2 Client Information

  • Full name, company, and a personal greeting (e.g., “Hi Sarah,”). Personalisation lifts response rates by up to 15 % according to HubSpot research.

2.3 Problem Statement (The Hook)

  • One to two sentences restating the prospect’s pain point. Use their exact words when possible; this shows active listening.

> Example: “You need a reliable payment gateway that can handle both local NGN transactions and international card payments without downtime.”

2.4 Solution Overview

  • Bullet‑point list of deliverables.
  • Include quantifiable benefits (e.g., “Reduce checkout abandonment by 22 %”).
  • Link to a case study or portfolio (internal link: `/case-studies/payment-gateway`).

2.5 Detailed Pricing Table

| Item | Description | Qty | Unit Price | Total |

|------|-------------|-----|------------|-------|

| Payment gateway integration | API + UI | 1 | ₦150,000 | ₦150,000 |

| Transaction monitoring dashboard | Custom UI | 1 | ₦80,000 | ₦80,000 |

| Subtotal | | | | ₦230,000 |

| VAT (7.5 %) | | | | ₦17,250 |

| Grand Total | | | | ₦247,250 |

  • Use alternating row colours for readability.
  • Highlight the Grand Total in a larger, bold font.

2.6 Terms & Conditions (Keep it short)

  • Payment terms – e.g., “50 % upfront, 50 % on delivery.”
  • Acceptable payment methods – integrate [Paystack](https://paystack.com/ or [Flutterwave](https://flutterwave.com/ links for online payment.
  • Delivery timeline.
  • Guarantee or support clause.

2.7 Call‑to‑Action (CTA)

  • Primary CTA: “Click Accept Quote to generate an invoice and pay instantly.”
  • Include a button that links to a payment page hosted on your site (use [Vercel](https://vercel.com/ or [Render](https://render.com/ for fast serverless deployment).
  • Add a secondary CTA: “Need changes? Reply to this email or schedule a call.”

2.8 Footer – Social Proof & Legal

  • Short testimonial (1‑2 lines) with client name.
  • Link to your **[Namecheap](https://www.namecheap.com/ domain privacy policy.
  • Optional: QR code that points to a live version of the quote for mobile viewing.

3. Designing for Clarity & Persuasion

Even the best copy can fall flat if the design distracts or confuses the reader.

3.1 Use a Clean, Responsive Template

  • Choose a PDF template that adapts to mobile screens. Around 60 % of decision‑makers review quotes on a phone or tablet.
  • Tools like [PandaDoc](https://www.pandadoc.com) and [Zoho Invoice](https://www.zoho.com/invoice/) provide ready‑made, responsive templates.

3.2 Typography & Color

  • Header fonts: Sans‑serif (e.g., Open Sans, 18‑22 pt) for modern feel.
  • Body fonts: Serif or sans‑serif at 11‑12 pt for readability.
  • Use your brand’s primary colour for headings and CTA button; keep body text black/gray for contrast.

3.3 Visual Hierarchy

  • Bold the most important numbers (price, discount, ROI).
  • Use icons next to each benefit – a check‑mark for “✅ 24/7 support”.
  • Add a progress bar graphic if the project has phases (Phase 1, Phase 2, Phase 3). It subconsciously signals a clear roadmap.

3.4 Trust Badges & Security Seals

  • If you accept online payments, embed the PCI‑DSS badge from Paystack/Flutterwave.
  • Include a Secure Socket Layer (SSL) seal (e.g., “Secured by Cloudflare”).

4. Leverage Technology & Automation

Manually drafting each quotation is a recipe for inconsistency and wasted time. Here’s a tech stack that streamlines the whole process.

| Need | Tool | Why It Helps |

|------|------|--------------|

| Quote creation & e‑signature | [HelloSign](https://www.hellosign.com) | Drag‑and‑drop fields, legally binding signatures, audit trail |

| Template management | [PandaDoc](https://www.pandadoc.com) | Central repository, version control, analytics on opens/views |

| Online payment collection | [Paystack](https://paystack.com/ / [Flutterwave](https://flutterwave.com/ | Instant payment links, automatic receipt generation |

| Hosting & fast delivery of PDFs | [Vercel](https://vercel.com/ or [Render](https://render.com/ | Serverless edge locations, zero‑downtime deployment |

| DNS & SSL | **[Namecheap](https://www.namecheap.com/ | Reliable domain management, free SSL certificates |

| CDN & DDoS protection | **[Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/ | Lightning‑fast asset delivery, security for payment links |

4.1 Step‑by‑Step Automation Workflow

1. Create a master template in PandaDoc with merge tags (e.g., `{{client_name}}`, `{{total_price}}`).

2. Integrate your CRM (HubSpot, Zoho CRM) via Zapier to pull client data automatically.

3. Trigger a Zap when a new deal reaches the “Quote” stage – it populates the template and emails the PDF to the prospect.

4. Include a Paystack payment button that auto‑generates an invoice using Paystack’s API.

5. Set up a webhook to notify your sales team the moment the client clicks Accept Quote.

6. Store the signed document in Google Drive or your own S3 bucket, linked back to the CRM record.

The entire process can be built in under 2 hours with no custom code, freeing you to focus on closing deals rather than paperwork.

5. Testing, Optimizing, and Follow‑Up

Even a perfect‑looking quotation won’t convert if you never test its performance.

5.1 Track Opens & Clicks

  • PandaDoc and HelloSign provide analytics on open rates, time spent, and CTA clicks. Aim for an open rate > 70 % and a CTA click‑through rate (CTR) > 30 %.

5.2 A/B Test Key Variables

| Variable | Variant A | Variant B |

|----------|-----------|-----------|

| Pricing presentation | Table with “Subtotal” highlighted | Pricing shown as “Package A – ₦247,250 (incl. VAT)” |

| CTA wording | “Accept Quote & Pay Now” | “Get Started Today” |

| Color of CTA button | Brand blue | Bright orange |

Run each test for 7‑10 days and let the data guide you.

5.3 Follow‑Up Cadence

1. Day 0 – Send the quotation.

2. Day 1 – Automated “Did you receive the quote?” email with a friendly reminder.

3. Day 3 – Share a relevant case study or ROI calculator.

4. Day 5 – Phone call or Zoom meeting to answer questions.

5. Day 7 – Final reminder with a limited‑time discount (e.g., 5 % off if signed within 48 hrs).

Consistent, value‑driven follow‑up boosts closure rates by up to 40 %.

📌 Actionable Takeaway

Create a “Quotation Playbook” that includes: a master template, a list of approved tools (PandaDoc, HelloSign, Paystack), a 5‑step automation flow, and a 7‑day follow‑up schedule. Review it weekly and tweak any element that falls below the benchmark (open rate < 70 % or CTA CTR < 30 %). This living document will become your conversion engine.
