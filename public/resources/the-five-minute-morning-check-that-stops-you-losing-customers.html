The 5-Minute Morning Check That Stops You Losing Customers

Most lost customers were never lost — they were unanswered

You open shop and the day swallows you. By 4pm, three enquiries sit unread, two warm leads have cooled, and a booking you forgot is now a no-show. None of these were disasters. They were just unseen. Five quiet minutes before the first customer fixes all of it.

The five things to check, in order

  1. Who went quiet? Send one follow-up nudge before anything else — quiet leads are the cheapest money you'll ever recover.
  2. Who replied overnight? Answer the warmest first; warmth decays by the hour.
  3. Who's booked today? Confirm the slots so "confirmed" stays confirmed.
  4. Who owes what? One polite payment reminder, sent before it's late.
  5. What must today produce? Pick the one thing, write it down, protect it.

Why the order matters

Quiet leads first because they already said maybe — a nudge converts them for free. New replies second because a fast answer is the difference between a sale and a stranger. Everything else can wait until the money that's already on the table is safe.
