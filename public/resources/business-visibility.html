Business Visibility: How Customers Find You on Google, AI Search and WhatsApp

By Elijah · AI Business founder · November 2026

Business visibility is the chance that a stranger who is ready to buy finds you, understands what you sell, and can reach you in one tap. It has three arenas: search engines like Google, AI assistants that answer questions directly, and the reach layer — WhatsApp, calls and listings — where interest becomes an enquiry.

Most owners work on only one arena. They chase Google rankings, then lose the customer who found them because there is no WhatsApp button. Or they post daily on social media while their website has no title, no description and nothing for an AI assistant to quote. Visibility is the whole chain, not one link in it.

The three arenas at a glance

  • Search visibility — can Google crawl, understand and show your pages?
  • AI visibility (AEO/GEO) — can an assistant quote you as the answer, with your name attached?
  • Reach visibility — can a ready buyer contact you in one tap, on the channel they already use?

What does search visibility actually depend on?

Search visibility depends on three things in order: whether a page can be crawled, whether its purpose is clear, and whether it answers the searcher better than the alternatives. Technical health comes first because a blocked or unreadable page cannot rank at all.

The foundations, in the order worth fixing

  • Crawlable. No accidental noindex, a reachable /sitemap.xml, and a robots.txt that points to it.
  • A title that names the service and the place. Roughly 30–60 characters: "Custom Cakes in Ikeja — Same-Day Orders | Sweet Crumbs".
  • A meta description of 120–160 characters that says what you do and what happens next. It is your ad copy in the results page.
  • One H1 stating the main service, then H2s for the questions customers actually ask.
  • Mobile viewport and HTTPS. Most Nigerian buyers arrive on a phone; a page that pinches and zooms loses them before the first sentence.
  • Speed. Compress images, cache assets. On mobile data, three seconds is the difference between an enquiry and a bounce.
  • Structured data. LocalBusiness, Service, FAQPage or Article JSON-LD turns your facts into something machines read reliably.
  • Alt text and internal links. Describe images; link related pages to each other so both readers and crawlers find the depth.

How much content does a page need?

A homepage needs a few hundred clear words: what you sell, who it is for, where you work, prices or price ranges, and how to contact you. A guide or article that should rank needs 600 words or more, because it has to answer the question fully and handle the follow-up questions. A page with almost no text — an empty JavaScript shell — gives a search engine nothing to understand.

What is AEO and GEO, and do they replace SEO?

AEO (answer engine optimisation) is making your content easy to lift as a direct answer. GEO (generative engine optimisation) is making your business a source that AI systems cite when they generate an answer. Neither replaces SEO. Google's own guidance is that its AI search experiences rest on the same search and content-quality foundations, and there is no special markup that guarantees inclusion.

So the honest position: you cannot buy or tag your way into an AI answer. You can make your pages the clearest, most attributable answer to a real question — which is the same work that wins ordinary search.

What makes content quotable by an AI assistant?

  • Answer first. Open each section with a 40–60 word direct answer, then explain. Assistants lift clean, self-contained statements.
  • Question headings. Use the exact phrasing customers type: "How much does a website cost in Lagos?" not "Pricing philosophy".
  • FAQPage and Article schema. Mark up real questions and answers, and give articles an author and a published date.
  • Visible authorship and freshness. Name who wrote it, their experience, and when it was last updated.
  • Consistent facts. Your business name, service and city should read identically on your site, your listings and your profiles. Ambiguity is the enemy of citation.
  • Server-rendered text. If your content only appears after JavaScript runs, many readers never see it. Put the substance in the HTML.
  • An llms.txt file. A plain-text map of your important pages at the site root. It is a courtesy to AI readers, not a ranking trick.

How does local visibility work in Nigeria?

Local visibility is how you show up when someone searches for a service near them. It rests on your Google Business Profile, your reviews, and consistent business details across the web. For a Nigerian business it usually produces enquiries faster than a national ranking, because the searcher is close enough to buy today.

What to complete on your Google Business Profile

  • Correct primary category, service area and opening hours — including public holidays.
  • Real photos of your work, your premises and your team, refreshed monthly.
  • Your services listed with prices or ranges, and your WhatsApp link in the contact field.
  • The same name, address and phone number format everywhere you appear.

Do reviews really change visibility?

Reviews influence both prominence and the decision to contact you. What matters is a steady flow of honest recent reviews and owner replies, not a single burst. Ask every satisfied customer once, make it a one-tap link, and reply to every review — including the unhappy ones. Our guide on getting more Google reviews covers the exact ask.

Why is the reach layer part of visibility?

Because a visitor who cannot contact you is, in practice, invisible. Most visibility audits stop at rankings and never check whether a phone visitor can call or message in one tap. In Nigeria the buying conversation happens on WhatsApp, so a wa.me link and a tel: link are conversion infrastructure, not decoration.

The one-tap test

  • Open your site on a phone. Can you message the business in one tap?
  • Can you call in one tap, without copying a number?
  • Does the message open with context, or an empty chat?
  • When that enquiry arrives, does anything remember it — or does it depend on your memory?

How do you measure visibility honestly?

Measure three layers separately: technical health, actual search demand, and enquiries received. A score alone tells you nothing about revenue. Verify your site in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools to see the real queries and clicks, then compare that with the enquiries that reached your WhatsApp.

What should you fix first if you have one hour a week?

Fix reach first, then clarity, then proof, then depth. Reach and clarity convert the visitors you already get; depth earns new ones over months. Doing it in that order means every week improves revenue rather than only rankings.

The order that pays

  • Week 1 — Reach. Add the WhatsApp button and a tap-to-call number. Test both on a phone.
  • Week 2 — Clarity. Rewrite the title and description with service + city. Add one H1.
  • Week 3 — Proof. Complete the Google Business Profile, add photos, ask five customers for reviews.
  • Week 4 — Depth. Publish one answer-first guide to a question customers ask you weekly.
  • Week 5 — Machines. Add LocalBusiness and FAQ schema, a sitemap and an llms.txt.
  • Week 6 — Measure. Verify Search Console, note the queries appearing, repeat what works.

How long does visibility take?

Technical and reach fixes show up in days, because they change what existing visitors do. Local profile improvements often show within weeks. Competitive search terms take months of consistent publishing, and nobody can honestly guarantee a position or an AI citation. Treat any promise of "guaranteed page one" as a warning sign.

Does visibility replace following up?

Frequently asked questions

What is business visibility in one sentence?

Business visibility is how easily a ready-to-buy stranger can find you, understand what you sell, and reach you in one tap — across search engines, AI assistants and local listings.

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

SEO earns positions in search results. AEO makes your content easy to lift as a direct answer. GEO makes your business a source AI systems cite. They share the same foundations: clear, crawlable, attributable content that answers a real question.

Do I need a website if I sell on WhatsApp?

You need at least one public page that states your service, city, prices and contact path, because that is what search engines and AI assistants read. A free business page is enough to start; a full site helps as you add guides.

Will llms.txt make ChatGPT recommend me?

No. llms.txt is a plain-text map that helps AI readers understand your site. It guarantees nothing. Being cited depends on how clear, consistent and useful your content is compared with other sources.

Why does my website show "Not scorable" in the check?

Because it serves JavaScript-rendered or reader-specific HTML, so the tool cannot see what a browser sees. Put your key content in the server HTML, then re-run the check.

What is the cheapest visibility fix that increases sales?

A WhatsApp button and a tap-to-call number on every page. They cost nothing and convert visitors you already have.
