Lead Finder Results, Decoded

The list is not a to-do list — it's a signal board

New owners read a lead list top to bottom and message everyone the same way. That's how you sound like spam. The chips next to each name are the point: they tell you where each conversation is, and each position has exactly one correct next move.

The map

ChipWhat it meansThe one move
🔴 quiet 4 daysThey went silent after contact — the deal is cooling right nowSend the drafted follow-up today, before it dies
🟡 no follow-upYou reached out once and never returnedSecond touch now — most deals close on touch 2 or 3
🟢 new enquiryFresh, warm, waiting on youReply within the hour; warmth decays fast
🟢 reachableContact verified, no conversation yetSafe to open with your first message
🟡 replied 2h agoLive conversation, they're engagedMove it forward: proposal, price, or booking — don't stall

Work the board, not the list

Each morning, handle the reds first (money cooling), then the fresh greens (money arriving), then the ambers (money stalling). Ten leads worked by signal beats fifty messaged in order — because the signal tells you who's ready now.

A lead list without statuses is a phone book. With statuses, it's a pipeline — and a pipeline tells you what to do next without you guessing.
