Your Business Page Is Your Public Trust Card

Before anyone pays you, they check you

Every enquiry has a silent step you never see: the moment they look you up. If they find nothing, or a page with no prices and no face, doubt wins. Your public Business Page is that check — and done right, it answers the four questions every stranger asks: who is this, where are they, are they open, what will it cost me?

The four things the page must answer

QuestionOn your page
Who?Business name + your name (a person, not a logo)
Where?Area and city — locals trust locals
Open?The open-now badge — availability at a glance
How much?Real prices on real services — kills the "how much?" ping-pong

Put the prices on the page

Owners hide prices fearing they'll scare people. The opposite happens: a priced page filters out the people who were never going to pay, and calms the ones who were. The stranger who sees ₦150,000 next to "property inspection" arrives already agreeing with your number — the conversation starts at yes instead of at negotiation.

Then put the page everywhere

WhatsApp bio, status footer, proposal signature, the link you paste when someone asks "where can I see your work?" One URL that answers all four questions, forever, while you sleep.
