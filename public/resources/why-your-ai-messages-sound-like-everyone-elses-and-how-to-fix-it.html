Why Your AI Messages Sound Like Everyone Else's — and How to Make Them Sound Like You

The tell: it reads like a template

Open any AI-written follow-up and you feel it instantly: polite, smooth, completely interchangeable. "I hope this message finds you well." Your customer has read that sentence forty times this week from forty other vendors. It doesn't sound professional — it sounds like everyone, which to a busy buyer means no one.

The problem isn't the AI. It's that the AI has never met you. It's writing from a generic idea of "a business," not from the way you actually talk to the woman who buys from you every Friday.

The fix: introduce yourself, once

AI Business has a Voice Profile — a short note about how you talk, saved once and injected into every proposal, caption, reply and follow-up the AI writes for you. Two minutes of writing beats a lifetime of editing templates.

Put this in itExample
Your tone"Warm and direct, like a friend who runs a shop — not a bank letter."
Words you never use"I never say 'Dear Sir/Madam' or 'best regards'."
How you sign off"I end with 'talk soon' and my first name."
How you talk price"Round numbers, no apologies, price stated once."
One real sentence of yoursPaste a message a customer loved, word for word.

Why this is the moat

Anyone can copy your price list. Nobody can copy the way you make a customer feel heard. The moment your outputs sound like you, switching tools means losing your voice — and that quiet gravity is why people stay.
