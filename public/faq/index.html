Frequently Asked Questions

Is AI Business free to use?

Yes. The Free plan costs ₦0 forever and includes 10 leads, 20 AI uses per month, and a basic dashboard. No credit card is needed to start.

Do I need a credit card to sign up?

No. You can create an account and start using the free plan immediately without entering any payment details.

Which countries does AI Business support?

AI Business is a web app that works anywhere with internet access. It is designed first for Nigeria, with pricing in Naira and WhatsApp-first workflows, and is used by entrepreneurs across Africa.

Does AI Business work with WhatsApp, Instagram and Facebook?

Yes. Customers you meet on WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, or in your shop can all be tracked in one place, and the AI drafts follow-up messages you can send through those channels.

What does the AI Receptionist actually do?

It sits on your free business page and answers common customer questions such as services, pricing, hours, and location 24/7, and captures every enquiry into your leads list even while you sleep.

Is my customer data safe?

Your data is stored securely and is never sold. AI processing uses contracted providers that do not train public models on your inputs. See the Privacy Policy for full details.

Can I cancel my paid plan anytime?

Yes. Paid plans are month-to-month and you can cancel anytime. The free plan remains available.

Do I need technical skills to use it?

No. Setup takes about 3 minutes: create an account, add your customers, and start using the tools from any phone or computer.
