Quick answers, no email needed. Tap a topic to open it.

🚀 Getting started in 5 minutes

1. Create your free account (no card needed).
2. Open Lead Finder and search your city + industry to get real prospects.
3. Save one lead to your CRM.
4. Use AI Tools to write your first message and send it on WhatsApp.
5. When they say yes, generate a proposal and mark the deal Won.

Finish these and your dashboard checklist lights up — that's your business running on a system, not on memory.

📥 Adding & managing leads

Add a lead from Customers (Leads) → + Add Lead, or save one straight from Lead Finder. Each lead keeps the name, phone, business, website, rating and your notes in one place.

Move leads through statuses: New → Contacted → Interested → Negotiation → Won / Lost. Your pipeline breakdown updates automatically.

⏰ Follow-ups: what they do (and don't do)

A follow-up date is a reminder for you. On that date, AI Business flags the lead and drafts a message you can send with one tap.

Nothing is ever sent to your customer automatically. You stay in control — the system remembers so you don't have to.

If a lead goes quiet for 3+ days, the Follow-Up Assistant lists them under "contact today" with a ready-to-send message.

📄 Proposals in 2 minutes

Open a lead → Generate Proposal. The AI writes a professional proposal using the details YOU provide (service, price, timeline) — no made-up facts. Edit anything, then share the link or download.

📅 Bookings & your public pages

Your Business Page is your free mini-website: services, gallery, reviews and a booking button. Share it on WhatsApp status and Instagram bio. Bookings land in Appointments where you confirm with one tap.

💳 Plans & getting paid

Free: 10 leads, 20 AI uses/day. Starter ₦6,000/mo, Pro ₦15,000/mo, Business ₦45,000/mo unlock more leads, AI power and automation. Your customers pay you via Paystack on proposals/invoices; you pay your subscription the same way.

🤝 Affiliate program: earn 50% + 20%

Share your referral link (menu → Affiliate). You earn 50% of the first payment of every customer you refer, plus 20% recurring while they stay subscribed. Track clicks and commissions in your affiliate dashboard.

❓ Quick FAQ

Is my data safe? Yes — stored securely, only you see your leads.

Does AI Business message my customers on its own? No. It drafts; you approve and send.

The site said "warming up" once — why? Free hosting sleeps after inactivity; it wakes in ~30s. Paid plans never sleep.

Can I export my leads? Yes — CSV export in the Leads page. Your data is yours.

Still stuck? Use 🆘 Support in your dashboard menu — it sends your issue straight to the founder.
