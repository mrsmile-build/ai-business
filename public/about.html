Our story

AI Business was founded by Elijah Ogunmuyiwa in Lagos, Nigeria, with a simple belief: business owners deserve practical technology that helps them find opportunities, manage customers, and run their businesses better.

I didn't start building AI Business from a technology office. I started with a phone, a strong desire to build something useful, and a lot to learn. I worked in construction during the day and spent my nights learning, experimenting, and building. But the goal was never simply to create another AI platform. I wanted to build practical tools that help businesses find customers, manage opportunities, and operate more effectively.

Today, AI Business is growing through real user feedback, continuous improvement, and a commitment to solving actual business problems. Every feature exists because a real user asked for it, starting with Precious, our first honest tester.

Vision

Every small business owner, whether in Lagos, Abuja, across Africa, or anywhere in the world, should have access to tools that help them grow without losing customers to silence.

Mission

Build practical, honest technology that helps WhatsApp-first businesses remember every customer, draft every message, and close more deals — without pretending to send anything without approval.

Values

Nothing sends without you

Every message is a draft you edit and approve. AI Business reminds and writes; you approve. Always.

Your data belongs to you

The leads you save, your notes, and your history stay in your account and on your device. We help you remember; we don't own your customers.

Real feedback, real features

Every feature exists because a real user asked for it. If something breaks, we read the message and fix it.

Honest about limitations

AI helps; it doesn't replace you. We never promise immunity from price rises, slow customers, or currency swings. We give you tools; you make the decisions.
