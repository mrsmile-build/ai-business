About AI Business

AI Business is a business software platform that helps small businesses find customers, manage enquiries, follow up automatically, book appointments, and get paid — all in one place.

Who it is for

AI Business is built for small business owners, solo entrepreneurs, and service providers — especially in Nigeria and across Africa — who sell through WhatsApp, Instagram, Facebook, or a physical shop and want a simple system to organise customers and grow revenue.

What it does

  • Lead Finder — find real local businesses and potential customers with contact details.
  • AI Receptionist — answers customer questions 24/7 on your business page.
  • Appointments — a free booking page so customers book themselves.
  • Follow-Up Assistant — AI drafts follow-up messages for every cold lead.
  • Invoicing — professional invoices in about 30 seconds, with payment tracking.
  • Free Business Page — a professional public page for your business in minutes.

Where it operates

AI Business is a web application available anywhere with internet access. It is designed first for the Nigerian market — pricing in Nigerian Naira and mobile-first, WhatsApp-friendly workflows — and is used by entrepreneurs across Africa.

What makes it different

  • Built specifically for African small businesses, not adapted from foreign CRMs.
  • A free forever plan with no credit card required to start.
  • Mobile-first and WhatsApp-friendly workflows.
  • AI that drafts real messages and answers real customer questions.
  • Everything in one place: leads, follow-ups, bookings, invoices, and a public business page.

Pricing

  • Free — ₦0 forever: 10 leads, 20 AI uses per month, basic dashboard.
  • Starter — ₦6,000/month: 50 leads, 40 AI uses, all AI tools, analytics.
  • Pro — ₦15,000/month: 500 leads, 80 AI uses, CSV export, full AI suite, priority support.
  • Business — ₦45,000/month: unlimited leads, 200 AI uses, team access, weekly AI report, onboarding call.

How to start

Create a free account on the homepage in about 30 seconds. No credit card is required. Add your customers, find new leads, and let the AI handle follow-ups.
