Questions owners actually ask

What is AI Business in one sentence?

A customer-keeping machine for WhatsApp-first businesses: it finds leads, drafts your messages, remembers every enquiry, and follows up so no sale goes cold.

I'm busy. What should I try first?

Lead Finder. Type your city plus your customer type; in about 30 seconds you get real businesses with phone numbers and ready-to-send WhatsApp messages. See it: Menu, then Lead Finder.

Does it message people without my permission?

Never. Every message is a draft you edit and send yourself. AI Business reminds and writes; you approve. Always. See it: any lead card has an editable message box.

What do the fire percentages mean?

Fit score: how strongly that business matches your offer right now, using rating, reviews and whether they have a website. Contact the hot ones first. See it: run any search and watch the badges.

What is the Memory badge on some leads?

Our collective memory. When any user searches a niche, AI Business remembers those businesses so the next person gets instant results without paid lookups. Your searches help everyone.

What reminds me about quiet leads?

The attention bell. Enquiries that go silent for days get flagged with a drafted nudge, so follow-up stops depending on your memory. See it: the bell icon at the top.

How do customers book me without back-and-forth?

Your free booking page: share one link, customers pick a day and time, it lands in your dashboard. No WhatsApp ping-pong. See it: Menu, then Booking page.

Can it write my proposals too?

Yes. Answer three quick questions and it drafts a clean proposal with your prices in naira, ready to send in about two minutes. See it: Menu, then Proposals.

How do I get more Google reviews?

The Review agent writes a warm please-review-us WhatsApp message for any happy customer, in your tone, in seconds. See it: the agents section in your menu.

Can it answer my customers for me?

Partly. The Receptionist agent drafts replies to common questions and the Off-hours agent writes your after-hours auto-reply. You stay the voice; it stops the silence.

I'm stressed and tired. Is that part of the app too?

Seriously, yes. Recharge starts a timed break with a book shelf, Word Scramble, Memory Pairs or a 2-minute story, and pauses all nudges until you are back. See it: the lightning button.

What happens when my internet dies?

A red banner tells you honestly, and your saved pages, stories and search history still open offline. Nothing pretends to work when it cannot.

Is there a free plan?

Yes, forever: 10 saved leads and 20 AI uses a month, no card needed. Starter, Pro and Business unlock more when you are ready. See it: Menu, then Subscription.

Can I earn with AI Business without selling my own service?

Yes. The Partner program: refer businesses you already help, keep 50 percent of their first payment and 20 percent every month they stay. See it: Menu, then Affiliate.

Where do my leads go if I close the app?

Nowhere. Saved leads, search history and stories live in your account and on your device. Tap a history chip and yesterday's contacts reopen instantly. See it: the chips under Find Leads.

Who built this?

A Nigerian founder who laid bricks by day and coded by night on a phone. Every feature exists because a real user asked for it, starting with Precious, our first honest tester.

Where do I see who is about to pay?

The Leads pipeline: six stages - New, Contacted, Interested, Negotiating, Won, Lost - with live counts. Change a stage in one tap and the numbers update. See it: Menu, then Leads.

Can I see how much I am actually making?

Analytics shows total leads, deals won, revenue from won deals, and your pipeline as percentages - so you see exactly which stage leaks money. See it: Menu, then Analytics.

How do I bring back old customers?

The win-back proposal template plus campaigns: pick past clients, AI writes a re-engagement offer with your prices, you approve and send it over WhatsApp. See it: Proposals, template menu.

Does it handle invoices and payments?

Yes. Professional invoices in about 30 seconds, and customers pay through Paystack links embedded in your proposals and invoices. See it: Menu, then Invoices.

What writes my social posts?

Moments: tell it your business and it drafts Instagram captions, WhatsApp status updates and LinkedIn posts in your voice. See it: Menu, then Moments.

Can it run a whole campaign, not just one message?

Campaigns queue outreach to many leads at once - AI drafts each message, you approve and send one by one on WhatsApp. Nothing ever sends without you.
