Your WhatsApp is a storefront you forgot to open

By Elijah · founder, AI Business · no fluff, no luck, just what actually worked

You already have a shop. It is the green chat on your phone. Customers walk in, ask the price, browse, and leave — and you have no sign, no opening hours, no record of who came, and no way to invite them back. The channel is perfect. The memory is missing.

Most Nigerian small business already lives on WhatsApp. The gap is never the channel — it is that the channel forgets. Give it a page people can find, a record of who messaged, and a follow-up that brings them back, and the same WhatsApp becomes a storefront that trades while you sleep.

The story behind this lesson

This is the whole reason the product is built the way it is. A free business page with your services and a booking button, shareable on your status and bio. A lead finder that puts real contacts in front of you. A follow-up that does not let a warm chat go cold. And a constellation of free tools — captions, invoices, proposals, reply generators — that all point back to the one engine that remembers your customers.

The insight is simple and it took me a year of building to see it: you do not need a website, a CRM, and a marketing agency. You need your WhatsApp to stop being a memory hole. Every piece here exists to do exactly that one thing, well.

Here is the evergreen truth people skip: a storefront without a record is just a window people walk past. The page brings them in, the record keeps them, the follow-up returns them. Open all three and the channel you already had starts selling like the one you wished you had.

Do this now (10 minutes)

Create your Business Page. Put two services and your price on it. Share the link on your WhatsApp status and your Instagram bio. Then save the first person who replies as a lead, so the storefront starts keeping score instead of just looking pretty.
